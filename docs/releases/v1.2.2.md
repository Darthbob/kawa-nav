The ride-settings card, made clear before anything goes to the bike: what a write sends, how Sport, Road and Rain come with their own power and KTRC, and profiles to keep the settings you use, as in Rideology. The bike's protocol is unchanged from 1.2.1, and the writes **have still not been tried on a bike**: check the dash after sending.

### New

- **All of it goes in one write, and the confirmation says so.** A ride-settings write always carries riding mode, power, KTRC and the quickshifter together; Rideology sends the whole set too, there is no way to send one value alone. The confirmation now lists every value next to what the bike has, by name: "Power: Full" for one that stays, "KTRC: 2 → 1" for a change, the engine brake "as the bike has it", and the name. It used to give the bike's values as raw numbers.
- **What you changed.** A picker that is not what the bike has is marked "changed". "Send to the bike" stays grey until something would change (a value, or the name since the last write), and "Back to the bike's values" puts the pickers back.
- **Sport, Road and Rain come with their own power and KTRC**, as Rideology's mode buttons set them: Sport Full / KTRC 1, Road Full / KTRC 2, Rain Low / KTRC 3. Picking one sets both; a Rider mode keeps yours.
- **Your own values go as Rider.** Change power or KTRC away from Sport, Road or Rain and the riding mode becomes Rider, with a note under it saying why. What a bike does with a preset mode and other values is not known, so your own values always go in the mode made for them. Values read from the bike never do this, and the quickshifter is no part of a preset.
- **Power and KTRC sit under the riding mode** they belong to: indented, with a line above them saying where they come from ("Sport: Full power, KTRC 1", or "Your own: Rider"). The quickshifter and the name follow under "Not part of the riding mode".
- **Profiles.** "Save as profile" keeps riding mode, power, KTRC and the quickshifter on the phone under the name in the Name field. The Profile picker at the top fills a profile in, ready to be checked and sent; "Delete profile" removes it from the phone only. Up to 20, kept with the settings Android backs up. The engine brake is not kept: a write sends it back as the bike has it.

### Not yet tried on a bike

Everything above is covered by unit tests (322 in the app; `SettingsProfilesTest` is new, `SettingsWriteTest` gained the presets, the switch to Rider and the confirmation's lines), but the card has not been on a phone with a real bike yet. Worth checking when you can, with the bike standing in neutral:

- pick Rain and see power and KTRC follow; pick Rider and see them stay;
- with Sport picked, change KTRC and see the mode move to Rider with the note;
- send, and compare the confirmation's lines and what the card reads back with the dash;
- save a profile, pick None and then the profile again, send it, delete it.

A report should include Settings → "Export the log and the capture" and what the dash showed.
