Two fixes around starting: updates are offered again, and the Bluetooth device you chose for autostart always starts KawaNav. How KawaNav talks to the bike is unchanged from 1.0.1.

### Fixed
- **A new version is offered when you open the app**: KawaNav looked for an update only once per start of its process, and Android keeps that process running for days (the notification access keeps it alive, even across a Force stop). 1.0.1 was therefore only offered after the phone had restarted. KawaNav now looks whenever you open it, at most every 6 hours, and again on the next open when it could not reach GitHub.
- **The Bluetooth device chosen for autostart always starts KawaNav** (Settings → Start automatically → "When my helmet connects": a helmet intercom, headset, …). After a Stop by hand it was ignored until the bike had been switched off or for up to an hour, so without the bike nearby it could not start KawaNav at all in that hour. The pause after Stop now applies to the bike only, where it keeps the bike from starting KawaNav again two seconds after Stop; the chosen device's connection ends the pause and starts KawaNav.

### Not yet tried on a bike
The update check was tried on a Pixel 9 (it runs when the app is opened), but not yet with a newer release waiting. The autostart from the chosen device after a Stop by hand has not been tried with a real helmet yet. Unchanged from 1.0.1: what the two traction-control pairs report and which rotation rate is which axis ([docs/PROTOCOL.md](https://github.com/Darthbob/kawa-nav/blob/v1.0.2/docs/PROTOCOL.md)), the dashboard's capability answer, writing riding modes 5-7 in the debug build and the byte 88 wheel-speed setting.
