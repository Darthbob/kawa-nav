KawaNav plans and navigates routes of its own now: from A to B or as a round trip, fastest, curvy or extra curvy, computed on the phone from the regions you downloaded. How KawaNav talks to the bike is unchanged from 1.2.9.

### New

- **Route planner** (Home → "Route planner", or Settings → "Navigation"). *A to B*: the start is your position or a point you tap, then a destination and via points on the map, or a place searched by name (needs a network). *Round trip*: a length from 30 to 400 km, a direction (or any) and a shape (triangle, square, pentagon, circle or any); "Another round trip" gives a different one. The line on the map shows how winding each stretch is: blue straight, green sweeping, orange winding, red very winding. Routes can be saved as GPX.
- **Curvy routes.** *Fastest* is close to the quickest way; *Curvy* prefers winding roads and stays out of towns, trading some time for bends; *Extra curvy* takes long detours for them. *Avoid motorways*, *Avoid tolls* and *Avoid gravel* (on by default) use such roads only when there is no other way. How winding a road is comes from its shape in the OpenStreetMap data: its change of direction per kilometre over about a kilometre of road, followed across the places where the map data cuts a road into pieces.
- **Navigation of its own.** The next maneuver goes to the dash with the same arrows as Google Maps' (roundabouts with their exit number, ramps, exits) and is shown on the phone, with the distance left and the arrival time. It is said in your helmet a few hundred metres before (from farther away on fast roads) and again at it, in the phone's language: English, Italian or German. Leave the route and a new one comes within seconds. With the curve warnings on, they follow the planned route. It runs with the screen off and without the bike; while it runs, Google Maps' directions are not sent to the dash.
- **Warnings in your language.** The spoken curve and speed camera warnings are in the phone's language too (English, Italian or German; any other language keeps English).

### Download your regions again

The route planner needs data that a region download now builds next to the road tiles (about as much storage again). Regions downloaded with an older KawaNav show "No route planning data yet" and **Update** under Settings → "Offline maps": download them once more.

### Not yet tried on a bike

The planner, the round trips and the navigation are covered by unit tests (439 in the app; 73 of them new) and were tried on the OpenStreetMap data of Andorra on a PC, but this version has not been on a phone yet. Worth checking: download a region again, plan a route and a round trip, ride a few turns and a roundabout with the navigation, and leave the route once. How winding a road counts and how much the curvy profiles give for it are first settings, to be tuned on real rides; very long routes in a large region may take a while to compute. A report should include Settings → "Export the log and the capture" and a screenshot of the planner.
