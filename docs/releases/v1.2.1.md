The bike's ride settings and the dash's shift lamp from the normal app, a speed camera that no longer sounds like a bend, and the dash's message icon for the messengers riders use. Writing to the bike is new outside the debug build and **has not been tried on a bike yet**: check the dash after sending.

### New

- **Ride settings and shift lamp in the normal app.** The bike screen can send a riding mode, power, KTRC and the quickshifter, and when the dash's shift lamp lights and at which engine speed, like Rideology's tuning screen; until now only the debug build could. The ride settings go out only with the bike connected, standing still, in neutral and with the throttle closed (Rideology only asks for standing still), after a confirmation that shows what is on the bike and what will be sent. The bike's answer is waited for and the settings are read back to compare. The suspension settings (KECS) stay read-only, as in Rideology.
- **What came of it.** The ride-settings card says whether a press would send now ("Ready", or "Not ready: the bike must stand still"). After a press both cards show for 30 seconds why it was not sent, that the bike gave no answer or refused it, or (for the ride settings) whether the settings it reads back are the ones sent.
- **A name for the ride settings.** An optional name (up to 18 bytes) goes with them, as Rideology sends the title of a setting. Whether the dash shows it anywhere is not known yet.
- **A speed camera sounds like a siren.** With "Beeps instead of words", a camera is now a two-tone siren, high-low twice in both ears, 640 ms long: through a mono intercom it can no longer be taken for the two beeps of a sharp bend. The bends beep as before.
- **Message icon for messengers.** The dash's mail/message icon also lights for WhatsApp (and Business), Telegram, Signal, Messenger, Threema, Outlook and Samsung's messages and mail, not only for Gmail, Google Messages and LINE; a WhatsApp, Telegram or Signal call lights the call icon. A notification that stays on ("WhatsApp Web is active", a backup, a call going on) lights nothing.

### For testers (debug build)

`KawaNav-v1.2.1-debug.apk` has three new tools in Settings → (tap the title 7 times) → "Bike data":

- **Test mark**: with "Capture frames" on, mark the moment of an action at the bike (indicators, high beam, clutch, side stand, a riding mode changed on the dash …). The capture then shows which bytes changed with it: the way to learn what the meter's status frame 0x30 (which Rideology ignores) and the undecoded bytes carry.
- **Dash notification test**: sends any of the notification categories the dash's protocol has (they are Apple's ANCS ones: missed call, social, schedule …), to see whether the dash shows an icon for the ones Rideology never sends.
- With "Requests after connecting" → Full and a capture, the frame timing shows whether the bike sends its riding data faster than once a second after the 0x08 init.

What these are and what is still unknown is in [docs/PROTOCOL.md](https://github.com/Darthbob/kawa-nav/blob/v1.2.1/docs/PROTOCOL.md), with a new section on what Rideology has but does not show.

### Not yet tried on a bike

Everything above is covered by unit tests (309 in the app) and was checked on an emulator with a replayed bike, but the writes have never reached a real dash. Worth checking when you can, with the bike standing in neutral:

- change the riding mode and KTRC, and compare the dash with what the card reads back;
- set the shift lamp and see it on the dash;
- try once while moving or in gear: the card must refuse;
- a WhatsApp or Telegram message and call with the dash icons on;
- the siren through the intercom.

A report should include Settings → "Export the log and the capture" and what the dash showed.
