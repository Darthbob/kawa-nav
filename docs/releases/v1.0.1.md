Ride distance, the bike screen's units and the warnings that fired too easily, fixed. How KawaNav talks to the bike is unchanged from 1.0.

### Changed
- **Distance from the odometer and the GPS together**: the dash's odometer reads whole kilometres, so a ride measured by it alone moved in 1 km steps and could be almost a kilometre off. The odometer now counts the kilometres and the GPS track fills in the part of a kilometre at each end of the ride; with only one of the two, that one alone. A recorded Z900 ride that read 13.00 km now reads 13.2 km (its track alone measures 13.4), and the CSV's `dist_m` no longer jumps 1000 m at each kilometre.
- **Rides saved before 1.0.1 are worked out again** when you open the log or the ride: their distance and average speed may change a little.
- **The bike's fuel economy unit is yours to choose** (Settings → On the road → Fuel economy): km/L, L/100 km, MPG US or MPG UK, L/100 km by default. The dash sends km/L whatever its own display shows; the bike screen now shows "5.2 L/100 km" instead of "19.2 (dash units)".

### Fixed
- **A second ride no longer starts with a jump**: a ride recorded after another, without restarting the app, started its distance with everything ridden in between.
- **Battery warnings fired too easily**: one low reading while the fuel pump primes, or just after a start, was enough. A warning now needs both readings the bike gives (its ECU and its dash) to be low, and the next answer from the meter 20 s later to say the same. Settings → On the road → Battery warnings turns them off, without touching tyre-pressure or ABS warnings.
- **No more −60 °C oil**: in the first seconds after connecting, before the bike says which values it has, a temperature it does not have was read as −60 °C (oil) or −40 °C (coolant, intake). Temperatures now wait for the bike's answer, and a reading at the very bottom of the scale counts as none. A cell like that in a ride saved by an older version is ignored when it is read back.
- **The instant fuel consumption's "no value" (102.3)** is dropped instead of recorded.
- **The phone battery icon on the dash no longer flashes low**: a single unreadable or impossible reading from Android (no value, or a drop no battery can make in five seconds) now keeps the last level shown.
- **Total time on the bike screen** is shown in hours and minutes: the dash reports minutes, not hours (4959 was 82 h 39 min, not 4959 h).

### Also
- The debug card names the IMU's roll and yaw rates; the third rate has no name yet.
- ride-report reads −40 / −60 °C cells from older exports as empty, so their charts no longer show a temperature that never was.

### Not yet tried on a bike
The distance, the battery-warning rule, the phone-battery guard, the total time and the fuel-economy units were checked on recorded rides and in the tests, not yet on a ride with 1.0.1. What the dash's own total-time display shows, and whether the fuel-economy setting matches the dash, would be good to confirm. Unchanged from 1.0: what the two traction-control pairs report and which rotation rate is which axis ([docs/PROTOCOL.md](https://github.com/Darthbob/kawa-nav/blob/v1.0.1/docs/PROTOCOL.md)), the dashboard's capability answer, writing riding modes 5-7 in the debug build, the byte 88 wheel-speed setting, and the Waze test while moving.
