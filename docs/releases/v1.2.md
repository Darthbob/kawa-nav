KawaNav speaks: the coming bend in your helmet, "slow down" when you come at a bend too fast for your pace, and the speed cameras if you want them. All of it is off until you switch it on, under Settings → On the road. How KawaNav talks to the bike is unchanged from 1.1.1.

### New

- **Spoken curve warnings.** "Sharp bend left", "Hairpin right": the kind of bend, as the dash's arrow shows it, and its side, about 6 seconds before the bend starts at your speed (50 m before it when slow, up to 300 m when fast). Pick hairpins only, sharp bends and hairpins, or all curves. Each bend is said once, nothing below 10 km/h, and at least 4 s between two warnings. It works with or without navigation; during navigation a bend at Maps' next maneuver is left to Maps' own voice. The voice is the phone's text-to-speech in English, played like Google Maps' voice: through the helmet intercom, with the music turned down while it speaks.
- **"Slow down" when too fast for your pace.** "Also when too fast" says any bend with "slow down" when you come at it more than 10% faster than your pace takes it: Relaxed, Normal, Sporty or Fast, which lean 20°, 28°, 35° and 42° through a bend. From the bend's radius that gives a speed — Normal takes a 100 m bend at 82 km/h — and the warning comes once gentle braking (about 0.3 g, after 2.5 s to hear it) would only just be enough. If you are already slowing down enough, it stays quiet.
- **A pace from your own rides.** "From my rides" leans as you do: every ride now counts its bends with the most lean you reached in each, and your usual lean is the middle one of the bends in your last 20 rides. It needs 30 bends (Normal until then), and Settings shows what it found: "You usually lean 27° in a bend: 143 bends in your last 12 rides".
- **The bend right after.** A bend starting within 80 m after the one said is named with it, "Hairpin left, then sharp bend right", and not said again on its own unless you come at it too fast.
- **Beeps instead of words.** One beep for a bend, two for a sharp one, three for a hairpin, in the ear of the bend's side, higher and quicker when you are too fast for it. A phone without text-to-speech beeps anyway.
- **Speed cameras by voice.** With the camera warnings on, "Say speed cameras" also says each one in the helmet ("Speed camera ahead, limit 50") or beeps it. The rules on camera warnings in your country apply as before: see the README's Speed cameras section.
- **Only through a headset.** By default nothing is said while no Bluetooth or wired headset is connected, so the phone does not talk out of a pocket. "Hear a curve warning" → Play plays one, to set the volume in the helmet.

### Rides

Every ride's summary now also counts its bends, for "From my rides". Rides recorded before this version are summarised again once, as after earlier updates: a few each time the ride list is opened, when a ride is opened, or when "From my rides" works out your lean. Nothing about the recorded values changes.

### Not yet tried on a bike

Everything above is covered by unit tests (301 in the app; `CurveAlertsTest`, `CurveBeepsTest` and `BendLeanTest` are new), but this version has not been on a phone or a bike yet. Worth checking when you can:

- that the voice reaches the intercom: an intercom connected only as a phone headset, without the music profile, may leave it on the phone's speaker;
- whether 6 seconds of warning are enough with the GPS and the speech delay, and how often it speaks on very twisty roads;
- whether the pace speeds feel right: the bends' radii come from the map data and are rough;
- whether "From my rides" finds a believable lean in your rides.

Every warning is written to the log ("Curve voice: Bend left, slow down, 110 m ahead at 90 km/h (radius 60 m, pace 64 km/h)"), so a report should include Settings → "Export the log and the capture" and where it happened.
