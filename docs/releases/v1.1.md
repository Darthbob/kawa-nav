A recorded ride shows much more of itself: the track on a map you can zoom, the torque and the rest of the recorded values in the charts, and a fuller summary. How KawaNav talks to the bike is unchanged from 1.0.4.

### New

- **The track lies on an OpenStreetMap map.** Pinch or double-tap to zoom, drag to move around, and the button at the corner of the card shows the whole ride again; a second picked on the chart that is out of sight brings the map to it, and at night the map is turned dark. The map is fetched only for the area on the screen and kept on the phone, as OpenStreetMap's tile policy asks (a User-Agent naming KawaNav, its cache headers honoured, nothing downloaded ahead); without a network the track is drawn on its own, as before. This is the one thing about a ride that leaves the phone: openstreetmap.org sees the area of the map you look at, not the track or any of the ride's values. Settings → "Map under the track" turns it off and nothing is fetched.
- **More of the ride in the charts.** The torque is one chart with three lines on one scale — what the throttle asks for, what the engine control asks for, what the engine gives — so the moments the electronics take torque away show as the lines parting. Also new: the roll and yaw rates (their scale is not established, see [docs/PROTOCOL.md](https://github.com/Darthbob/kawa-nav/blob/v1.1/docs/PROTOCOL.md)), the GPS speed, the fuel use, the wheelie angle, the rear wheel's slip against the front (not below 10 km/h) and, marked unverified, the suspension speeds and the raw brake values. Touching a chart names the picked value under it, and each value has its own decimals on the axis.
- **A fuller summary.** The folding sections add the body's accelerations forward, sideways and up, the lean either side, the front and rear wheel speeds, the GPS speed, the instant fuel economy in km/L, the torque, the fuel use, the suspension speeds and the raw brake values; the rotation rates and the rear wheel slip show from lowest to highest, as their average says nothing. The engine section gains the time in each gear and the gear changes, with those the quick shifter made. Rides saved by an older version get the new rows worked out once, when they are opened or listed.

A value only shows up when the bike sends it: a Z900, for one, sends no torque, no suspension speeds and no raw brake values.

### Not yet tried on a bike

Everything above is covered by unit tests (249 in the app; `MapTilesTest` and `ChartViewTest` are new, `RideTest` gained the new summary rows), and one map tile was fetched with the app's own User-Agent, but this version has not been on a phone yet. Worth checking when you can: open a recorded ride — the map should appear under the track, zoom and drag should move it while the page still scrolls outside it, and the map should go dark at night; compare the engine section's time in each gear and its quick-shifter count with the ride you remember. A report should include Settings → "Export the log and the capture" and a screenshot of the ride. Unchanged from 1.0.4: the values and warnings, what the two traction-control pairs report, which rotation rate is which axis ([docs/PROTOCOL.md](https://github.com/Darthbob/kawa-nav/blob/v1.1/docs/PROTOCOL.md)), the dashboard's capability answer, writing riding modes 5-7 in the debug build and the byte 88 wheel-speed setting.
