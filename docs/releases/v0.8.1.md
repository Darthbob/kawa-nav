The ride exports now carry everything a ride records. Nothing else changes: how KawaNav talks to the bike is the same as in 0.8.

### New
- **CSV (all values)**: the ride as a table with one column per recorded value — the suspension travel and its speeds, the three accelerations, the three rotation rates, the filtered wheel speeds, the traction-control and quick-shifter flags, the torques, the brake pressures, the ABS state, damping, cruise control, the wheel-speed setting, plus everything the other export has. A comment line on top says which ride it is, when, how far and how long.
- **Raw file**: the same values in the app's own file format, for anyone who wants to read them with a script rather than a spreadsheet.

Both appear under "Keep or share" on a ride, next to the existing exports, and both go to `Download/KawaNav`. Rideology's CSV (fourteen columns, its own layout) and the GPX track are unchanged.

### Why
Until now the CSV carried Rideology's fourteen columns and the GPX thirty of the sixty-three values a ride records: the rest could only be read with a cable and `adb`. A ride recorded on the bike is the rider's data, and all of it is now exportable from the phone.

### Not yet tried on a bike
Unchanged from 0.8: what the newly recorded values mean against the dash (suspension travel and its unit, which IMU axis is which, the byte 88 wheel-speed setting), the capability answer on a real meter, and writing riding modes 5-7 (debug build). The exports themselves are checked on the emulator: both files written, sixty-three columns, header and rows aligned.

### Which APK
- **`KawaNav-v0.8.1.apk`**: the app for everyday use.
- **`KawaNav-v0.8.1-debug.apk`**: the same app with the test tools and the guarded writing of ride settings and shift lamp; tap the Settings title seven times. Both are signed with the same key, so one installs over the other and back, keeping rides, refuels, battery readings, the maintenance log and settings.

### Install / update
Open the APK on the phone (Android 13+). It updates 0.8 in place (and anything from v0.4 on) and keeps everything those versions saved. Known limitations: [README](https://github.com/jackyes/kawa-nav/blob/v0.8.1/README.md#-known-limitations).
