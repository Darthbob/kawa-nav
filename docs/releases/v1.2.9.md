A fix for the phone battery warning that flashed on the dash while navigating (issue #7).

### Fixed

- **Phone battery icon.** While you navigated, the dash could show its empty-battery warning for a while and then the right bars again, with the phone well charged and not on a charger. KawaNav never sent the dash a low or unknown level: the dash ignored many of the icon updates, because the arrow came right behind them before the dash had taken the icons. With too few updates it showed the warning. KawaNav now sends the next frame to the dash only once the dash has confirmed the last one, as Rideology does.

### Not tried on a bike yet

- The fix comes from the logs of rides on two Z900s: while navigating, the dash confirmed only one icon update in two or three, and every one without the arrow. Whether the warning is gone has not been seen on a ride yet.

A report should include Settings → About → "Export the log": it now says when the dash stops confirming a kind of frame ("Meter did not confirm …").
