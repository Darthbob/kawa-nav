Fixes the time of recorded rides. How KawaNav talks to the bike and the look are unchanged from v0.5.

### Fixed
- **Ride time counted twice**: every minute of a ride was counted again, so a ride's time came out too long, its average speed too low, and the charts' time axis was stretched. Rides recorded with v0.4 or v0.5 keep the values they were saved with.
- **Pause and continue**: pausing dropped the time since the last start or continue, and continuing subtracted the pause a second time. Pauses are now simply left out.
- **Fuel used** at the end of a ride was added on top of the value already saved during the ride.

### Which APK
- **`KawaNav-v0.5.1.apk`**: the app for everyday use.
- **`KawaNav-v0.5.1-debug.apk`**: the same app with the test tools and the guarded writing of ride settings and shift lamp; the test cards are in Settings, above About (tap the Settings title seven times). Both are signed with the same key, so one installs over the other and back, keeping rides and settings.

### Install / update
Open the APK on the phone (Android 13+). It updates v0.4 and v0.5 in place and keeps rides, settings and offline maps. It installs next to Darthbob's KawaNav, not over it: keep only one of them connected to the bike. Not yet tried on a bike: see [Known limitations](https://github.com/jackyes/kawa-nav/blob/v0.5.1/README.md#-known-limitations).
