The riding log now shows the numbers the rides started keeping in 0.9. How KawaNav talks to the bike is unchanged from 0.9.1.

### New
- **Records from the values a ride keeps**: the hottest engine, the hardest braking (the strongest deceleration of any ride) and the most suspension travel used — each with the date of the ride it happened on, next to the fastest ride, the most lean and the longest ride that were already there.
- **What the whole log came to**: the totals line adds the fuel burned and the consumption over all the rides, and a second line adds up the time the electronics spent working — traction control, ABS, and the front wheel up.

### Changed
- **A ride saved before 0.9 gets its numbers when you open the log**: five at a time, in the background, on the store's own thread. It never touches a ride that is still being recorded, and the list fills in over a couple of visits rather than all at once. On the emulator's seven test rides the first visit upgraded five and the records appeared.

### Not yet tried on a bike
Unchanged from 0.9.1: what the two traction-control pairs report and which rotation rate is which axis (both now have the tests written down in [docs/PROTOCOL.md](https://github.com/jackyes/kawa-nav/blob/v0.9.2/docs/PROTOCOL.md)), the dashboard's capability answer, writing riding modes 5-7 in the debug build, the byte 88 wheel-speed setting, and the Waze test while moving. The records added here are computed from the phone's own files and never touch the bike.

### Which APK
- **`KawaNav-v0.9.2.apk`**: the app for everyday use.
- **`KawaNav-v0.9.2-debug.apk`**: the same app with the test tools and the guarded writing of ride settings and shift lamp; tap the Settings title seven times. Both are signed with the same key, so one installs over the other and back, keeping rides, refuels, battery readings, the maintenance log and settings.

### Install / update
Open the APK on the phone (Android 13+). It updates 0.9.1 in place (and anything from v0.4 on) and keeps everything those versions saved. Known limitations: [README](https://github.com/jackyes/kawa-nav/blob/v0.9.2/README.md#-known-limitations).
