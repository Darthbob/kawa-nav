The dash's low fuel warning, older Kawasaki dashes recognized, and the supported models sorted by the meter they have, all from Rideology's October update (2.11.14).

### New

- **Low fuel.** Rideology 2.11.14 reads a new value of the dash: its low fuel warning (byte 31 of the 0x41, announced by the 0x40). KawaNav reads it too: "Low fuel" next to the fuel bars on the Bike tab, and a "Fuel is low" warning with the range left, on Home and as a notification, like the tyre and ABS warnings. A 2026 Z900 does not send it (its dash says so), so on a Z900 nothing changes.
- **Older dashes.** A Kawasaki whose meter is not BLE5 (most 2019-2024 Rideology bikes, the Ninja 1100SX, Versys 1100, Z650 S, the 2027 Ninja 650 and Ninja H2) cannot show directions, and KawaNav cannot talk to it. It is now recognized by its Bluetooth services: Home says "Older Kawasaki dash: not supported" and KawaNav stops trying to connect, instead of searching over and over.

### Changed

- **Supported models.** The README and docs/MODELS.md now sort the models by the meter Rideology's own model list gives them: BLE5 (found by the VIN) or older (found by the meter's part number). The Ninja 1100SX, Versys 1100, Z650 S and the 2027 Ninja 650 / Ninja H2 move from "unclear" to "not supported"; the Z500 / Ninja 500, KLX230, KLE500 and the new KLX300 / KLE650 are BLE5, with turn-by-turn not known.

### Not tried on a bike yet

- The low fuel warning has been checked against Rideology's code and the Z900's real frames, not on a bike that sends it.
- The older-dash check has been tested on the JVM only: no such bike was at hand.

For developers: docs/PROTOCOL.md has the comparison of Rideology 2.11.14 with 2.11.9 (the only protocol change is the low fuel warning) and a new section on the meters before BLE5.
