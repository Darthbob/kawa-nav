The bike's IMU worked out: a Bosch MM7.10, mounted at 45°. Its rotation rates are now the bike's yaw, roll and pitch, in degrees a second.

### Changed

- **Yaw, roll and pitch rates.** The IMU sends its rates about its own axes, and two of them sit at 45° like its accelerations. KawaNav showed one leg of that pair as the yaw rate, about a third too low, and the other as the roll rate, which was half yaw. It now turns them into the bike's directions, as it already did for the accelerations: the yaw rate gives each bend's change of course as the GPS measures it (1.01× over 330 bends of six rides), and the third rate, "IMU rate 1 (unverified)" until now, is the pitch rate. On the Bike tab, in the ride charts, in the ride summary and in the ride report, in °/s; rides already recorded are summarised again once. Yaw and roll count + to the right; which sign of the pitch rate is nose up is not established yet.
- **IMU readings with a broken checksum are left out.** Each IMU message carries a checksum; the rare one that does not add up (2 in 40,000 on a Z900) no longer reaches the lean, the accelerations or the rates.

### Not tried on a bike yet

- The new Bike tab rows and charts were checked against recorded rides, not watched on the phone during a ride.

### For testers (debug build)

- The debug card shows the IMU's message counter, how many of its messages failed the checksum and how many came twice, since connecting.
- "Engine start" and "Indicator left" are off the Test mark list: both were tried. The left indicator is in none of the frames.
- Field tests → "Which way the pitch rate counts": push the front down with the capture on, to settle the pitch rate's sign.
