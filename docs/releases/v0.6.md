Speed camera warnings from the offline map data — optional, off by default. How KawaNav talks to the bike is unchanged from v0.5.1.

### New
- **Speed camera warnings** (Settings → "On the road"): the OpenStreetMap extract of a downloaded region holds the speed cameras (fixed, red-light, average-speed), and KawaNav reads them like the roads. While riding, the app shows a card and a notification with sound when a camera is within the chosen distance (300, 500 or 800 m), and the dash gets the distance — it has no camera icon, so it uses its destination flag. While navigating, the next turn is only taken over within 250 m, so the maneuver stays visible longer. Nothing is asked of a server and nothing is uploaded.
- **Download your regions again** to get camera data: a region downloaded before this version has none, and the app says so on the region (its **Update** button is offered for that even when the road data is up to date).

### Changed
- Debug builds only: a hidden "Notifications" card in Settings records what other apps put in their notifications, for checking a navigation app that cannot be read like Google Maps (Waze) on a real phone first. It copies notification text, so it stays off until it is switched on, and release builds cannot capture at all. How to use it: [docs/WAZE.md](https://github.com/jackyes/kawa-nav/blob/v0.6/docs/WAZE.md).

### Not yet tried on a bike
Everything about the cameras: the settings and the phone side are checked on an emulator with a real region (Liechtenstein, 12 cameras) and a simulated approach, but nobody has ridden past a camera with the dash connected yet — in particular what the destination flag with the distance looks like there. The camera data itself is only as good as OpenStreetMap: incomplete, sometimes outdated, and **not a radar detector**. Announcing cameras while driving is illegal in some countries (Germany, France, Switzerland): check the rules where you ride.

### Which APK
- **`KawaNav-v0.6.apk`**: the app for everyday use.
- **`KawaNav-v0.6-debug.apk`**: the same app with the test tools (hidden cards: meter icons, bike data, notification capture) and the guarded writing of ride settings and shift lamp; tap the Settings title seven times. Both are signed with the same key, so one installs over the other and back, keeping rides and settings.

### Install / update
Open the APK on the phone (Android 13+). It updates v0.4, v0.5 and v0.5.1 in place and keeps rides, settings and offline maps. It installs next to Darthbob's KawaNav, not over it: keep only one of them connected to the bike. Known limitations: [README](https://github.com/jackyes/kawa-nav/blob/v0.6/README.md#-known-limitations).
