Recorded rides: a ride now ends when the bike is switched off, its fuel figures are right, and "Stop ride" no longer does nothing after KawaNav stopped. The bike's protocol is unchanged from 1.2.2.

### New

- **A ride ends when the bike is switched off.** Switching off cuts the link, the ride pauses, and once the bike has been gone 20 seconds the ride ends and its summary comes up in a notification. It used to end only when KawaNav stopped, 5 minutes after an autostart, and never when KawaNav had been opened by hand: then the ride stayed paused and carried on the next time the bike was switched on, even the next day.
- **Settings → "End a ride"**: At once (the default), After 5 min or After 15 min. With a wait, switching the bike on again in time continues the same ride, so a fuel stop does not split it in two.
- **A dropout is not the ignition.** KawaNav goes by the speed the bike had when the link went: moving at 5 km/h or more, the ride waits at least 5 minutes for the bike, whatever the setting. A ride you paused yourself is left alone.
- **Fuel used and L/100 km on a bike without a fuel counter** (the Z900): estimated from the instant fuel economy, each second burning the distance it covered over its km/L, and marked "≈" on the ride, in the notification and in the riding log's totals. Seconds without an economy (engine braking, standing still) burn nothing, so idling is not in it.

### Fixed

- **"Stop ride" that did nothing.** When KawaNav stopped while a ride was being recorded (Stop in the notification, "Stop KawaNav" on Home, or by itself 5 minutes after the bike was switched off), the ride was saved, but the record button stayed red or amber and Home kept "Recording the ride". Tapping "Stop ride" or "Pause" then reached no service and nothing happened. The screens now go back to idle when KawaNav stops, and a tap on a state left behind clears it. Reproduced and checked on the emulator.
- **The instant fuel economy's average** is the distance over the litres, no longer the average of its seconds. That counted a second at walking pace like one at 120 km/h, and the 99.9 km/L of a closed throttle lifted it: on five Z900 rides it read 20–26 km/L where the fuel gives 13–18 (5.6–5.9 L/100 km on the road, 7.4 in town). Saved rides are worked out again when the list or the ride is opened.
- **The riding log's total consumption** divides the litres by the kilometres of the rides that know their fuel, no longer by every ride's, including those recorded without the bike.

### Not yet tried on a bike

The fuel figures were checked on the five recorded rides, and the new end of a ride is covered by unit tests (`RideEndTest`), but neither has been on a phone with a real bike yet. Worth checking when you can:

- switch the bike off at the end of a ride and see the summary come about 20 seconds later;
- with "After 5 min", stop for less than 5 minutes and see the same ride go on.

A report should include Settings → "Export the log and the capture".
