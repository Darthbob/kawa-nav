KawaNav in nine languages, a theme setting and a green light theme, the fuel gauge's reserve like on the dash, and a route planner that says when it has no map.

### New

- **Nine languages.** Every screen and the voice (navigation, curve, speed camera and cold engine warnings) in English, German, Italian, French, Spanish, Dutch, Portuguese, Polish and Swedish. KawaNav follows the phone's language when it has it, else English; Settings → Appearance → Language picks one for KawaNav alone ("System default" goes back to the phone's). The translations were written with AI help: corrections are welcome.
- **Theme.** Settings → Appearance → Theme: "Like the phone" (as before, the default), "Light" or "Dark", whatever the phone's dark mode is. Every screen, dialog and the status bar follow it at once.
- **Reserve on the gauge.** When the dash's last fuel bar blinks (reserve), Home and the Bike tab show it the same way: one orange blinking bar and "Reserve" instead of "0 bars".

### Changed

- **Green light theme.** The light theme is green instead of violet: buttons, switches and the fuel gauge in the lime green of the dark theme, values and text in a darker green that stays readable in sunlight.
- **Refuels from reserve.** A fill from reserve is recorded in the refuel diary; before, a gauge at 0 was not compared at all.
- **Route planner.** It says at once when there is no map to plan on and takes you to Offline maps; it finds your position with Wi-Fi and cell towers too; its map shows the border of each downloaded region.

### Not tried on a bike yet

- The voice in the new languages (French, Spanish, Dutch, Portuguese, Polish, Swedish) has been tested on the JVM only, not in a helmet.
- The reserve on the gauge has been shown on a phone with a test value, not with a bike on reserve.
- A fill from reserve has been tested on the JVM only.
