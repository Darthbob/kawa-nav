Maintenance reminders as Rideology shows them, a crash in the log, less battery without the bike, and the distance to a bend measured to the bend.

### Fixed

- **Maintenance reminders.** The Kawasaki service is due at an odometer reading, so it is shown "at 12,000 km (1,050 km left)" like Rideology, not added to the odometer; a reminder switched off on the dash says "off" instead of the dash's default date; and the names of the reminders no longer disappear when the phone writes dates day first.
- **Curves.** The distance to a bend was the distance to the smoothing window around it: 15 m before a real bend, 30 m before a bend drawn as a single corner in the map. It is now the distance to where the road starts to turn, within 5 m.
- **Straight ahead.** The road ahead was measured up to one map segment beyond the look-ahead; it is now cut at the look-ahead exactly.

### Changed

- **A crash is written into the log** before the app closes, so Settings → About → "Export the log" carries it with what led up to it. Attach it to a report.
- **GPS off while the bike is away.** Started by hand and left running without the bike, KawaNav switches the GPS off after 5 minutes unless a ride is being recorded or a navigation runs (Home says "Waiting for the bike (GPS off)"), and on again the moment the bike connects. An automatic start is not affected: it stops itself after those 5 minutes as before.
- **Converting a downloaded region** sizes its work to the memory the phone gives the app: fewer rounds and a faster conversion on phones with more, no out-of-memory on phones with less.
- The README now says exactly which road free ride follows at a junction: the one keeping your road's name or number, as long as it does not turn more than 45° further than the straightest road on; otherwise the straightest, with a preference for roads of the same or a higher class. That is how it has always worked; the README said less.

### Not tried on a bike yet

- The GPS pause and the wake lock that is now renewed every minute (it used to be held without a timeout) have been tested on the JVM only. On a ride, nothing should change; after the bike has been off for 5 minutes, Home should say "Waiting for the bike (GPS off)" and the arrow and curves should be back at once when the bike is switched on again.

For developers: the dash decision is now in `DashArbiter` (plain Java) and the curve detector and the road following have tests of their own (64 new tests); lint warnings fail the build.
