The page for one ride now shows what the ride actually recorded, folded into sections so it stays readable. How KawaNav talks to the bike is unchanged from 0.8.1.

### New
- **Every recorded value, over the whole ride**: the page keeps six tiles at the top (time, average speed, top speed, max lean, **max engine speed**, fuel used) plus the fuel economy, and under them five sections — Engine, Chassis and suspension, Brakes and electronics, Temperatures, Data quality — each with the **lowest, average and highest** of every channel it holds: engine speed, throttle, front and rear suspension travel, acceleration, wheelie angle, tyre pressures, front brake pressure, battery, the four temperatures, GPS accuracy and altitude.
- **How long the electronics worked**: traction control, ABS, cruise control and the front wheel coming up are reported as time ("cut in for 12 s", "never"), not as an average — a level averaged over a ride says nothing.
- **Sections fold away** (tap the title), each showing its highest value while closed, so the page opens with the numbers a rider looks at first.
- **Data quality**: what was recorded and how completely (seconds recorded against seconds of the ride), the odometer, and both distances when the bike's odometer and the GPS track disagree.

### Changed
- **The ride summarises itself once**, when it ends, and the numbers are saved in the ride's own file. Opening a ride is instant, and a ride saved by an older version gets its numbers the first time it is opened.
- **The track and the value chart are 40 dp shorter**: the numbers are the point of the page and the two pictures follow them.
- **The chart card no longer needs speed or engine speed**: a ride with only altitude (say, recorded from the phone alone) shows its chart too. It used to stay hidden.
- Values the bike never sent are left out rather than shown as zero, and a real zero (no braking, no throttle, upright) still shows.

### Fixed
- A ride recorded from the phone alone used to show no chart at all.

### Not yet tried on a bike
Nothing new in this release touches the bike: the summary is computed from the file on the phone. Unchanged from 0.8: what the newly recorded values (suspension travel, IMU axes, byte 88) mean against the dash, how the dash answers the capability question, and writing riding modes 5-7 (debug build). The "Not yet tried" list of 0.8 still stands.

### Which APK
- **`KawaNav-v0.9.apk`**: the app for everyday use.
- **`KawaNav-v0.9-debug.apk`**: the same app with the test tools and the guarded writing of ride settings and shift lamp; tap the Settings title seven times. Both are signed with the same key, so one installs over the other and back, keeping rides, refuels, battery readings, the maintenance log and settings.

### Install / update
Open the APK on the phone (Android 13+). It updates 0.8.1 in place (and anything from v0.4 on) and keeps everything those versions saved. Known limitations: [README](https://github.com/jackyes/kawa-nav/blob/v0.9/README.md#-known-limitations).
