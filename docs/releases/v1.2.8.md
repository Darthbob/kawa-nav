How a recorded ride's bends were ridden, maintenance reminders of your own, a cold engine warning, and Google Maps' Italian checked on real rides.

### New

- **Bends of a recorded ride.** A Bends card on the ride screen: how many bends there were, the typical lean, apex speed and speed out against in; how many were braked at the apex, had the throttle snapped open while leant over, or had a downshift in them; a diagram of the grip the ride used, a dot for every riding second (sideways from the lean, forward and back from the change of speed); and the bends by most lean, fastest, best exit, most speed lost or in ride order, each with its apex speed, the radius that speed and lean give, the speeds in and out and the throttle at the apex. Tapping one shows it on the track and the chart. Checked against an analysis of four Z900 rides made on a computer: the same bends, with the same leans, radii and speeds.
- **Maintenance reminders of your own.** On the Maintenance screen: a job every so many kilometres or months, whichever comes first, counted from the last time it is logged (the chain every 1,000 km, the oil every 6,000 km or 12 months). Each shows what is left, "soon" when it is close and "due" once it is past; a notification says when one is due, and the bike screen lists it.
- **Cold engine warning** (Settings, off by default): "Engine still cold" in the helmet when you rev the engine above 4,500 rpm before the coolant reaches 70 °C, or two long low tones with "Beeps instead of words". Both limits can be changed. Once the coolant has been warm it stays quiet until the next connection.

### Changed

- **Google Maps in Italian** is checked on nearly 3,000 real notifications. A roundabout called "rotatoria" and a sharp turn ("Svolta tutto a sinistra") are now read from the text too, not only from Maps' arrow.

### Not tried on a bike yet

- The cold engine warning has not been heard on a ride: its rule is tested, and it speaks with the curve warnings' voice.
- The Bends card and the reminders were tried on the emulator with recorded rides, not on a phone after a ride.
