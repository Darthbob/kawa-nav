Fixes from the rides of 17–25 October: battery warnings at every start, the dash's maintenance distances, curves that flipped on the dash, and the wrong bike with two Kawasakis paired.

### Fixed

- **Battery at the start.** The 12 V reading taken with the engine still off (11.5–12 V) was judged as a charging voltage once the engine started, so "Battery is not being charged" came up at every start while the charging system was fine. A reading now counts only when it was taken at least 5 s after the engine started or stopped.
- **The 12 V battery log** keeps a resting reading only with the engine off for half an hour, and none while the starter turns. It had kept 13.8–14.3 V just after a stop and 11.1 V during a start. Readings of 13.6 V or more already in the log are left out.
- **Maintenance.** The distance the dash keeps for each reminder counts down with the odometer: it is what is left, not the odometer the service is due at. The Bike tab now shows "in 11,045 km (at 16,597 km)", and "reminder off" for a reminder the dash keeps switched off.
- **Curves stop flipping.** A bend at the edge of two kinds, a bend found on one GPS fix and not the next, or the bends just after a roundabout could change the dash arrow back and forth every second. A change is now shown once it is confirmed; a bend that comes up is still shown at once.
- **Two Kawasakis paired.** KawaNav connects to the bike "Start automatically" is set up with. It used to take the first paired Kawasaki and wait for ever when that was the other bike. With several paired and none picked, the bike status says how to pick one.

### For testers (debug build)

- Switching "Capture frames" on with the bike connected starts a capture at once; it used to wait for the next connect, and "Mark now" said no capture was open.
- Field tests → "The dash's maintenance reminders": compare the Bike tab with the dash's own maintenance page, to settle what the other reminders mean.
