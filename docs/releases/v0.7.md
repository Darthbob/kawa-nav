Values the meter sends that KawaNav had never shown, a ride diary kept on the phone, and the corrections that came out of checking every one of them against Rideology. How KawaNav talks to the bike changed in two ways, both read-only.

### New
- **Refuels** (Bike → Refuels). The bike never says how many litres went in, so a fill is noticed as the gauge and the range jumping up while the bike stands still, and the litres are estimated from the tank size you set (default 15 L, change it to your bike's). The screen lists the fills with the fuel used between them and lets you correct the litres, add a note, or add a fill by hand. Standing at the pump with KawaNav connected, it looks after itself.
- **12 V battery** (tap the battery figure on the Bike screen). One reading per stop, taken with the engine off — a running engine's 13–14 V hides everything. The screen charts the readings and says when the battery has lost enough to be worth checking; the app tells you when that happens, once a day at most.
- **Maintenance log** (Bike → Maintenance). What you had done, when, and at what odometer, next to the reminders the dash keeps.
- **Records** (Riding log). Fastest ride, most lean each way, longest ride, and the distance per year — once you have rides to compare.
- **A ride summary** when a ride ends: distance, time, fuel used, best lean, top speed, on a notification that opens the ride.
- **More of what the bike already sends** is now shown: torque asked for and given, the brake pressures, the ABS target and indicator, where the electronic suspension stands, the three rotation rates, the filtered wheel speeds, the suspension unit's traction-control level and quick-shifter state. All on the Bike screen, all labelled as not yet compared with a dash where that is the case.

### Changed
- **The riding log extension (0x45) is now asked for on every connect**, as Rideology does. Until now KawaNav only asked for it at the debug "full" level, so temperatures, fuel and tyre pressures arrived only with that switch on. If a release after this one shows no temperatures or tyre pressures, the debug build has a switch to go back to asking only at ride start.
- **A second notification channel** ("Rides and refuels") for the ride summary and the refuel
  confirmation: neither is a warning, and the two can now be silenced apart from one another.
- Refuels, battery readings and the maintenance log are included in Android's backup and in the
  phone-to-phone transfer, like the rides.
- Warning texts, the debug card and the protocol document were corrected where they disagreed with the meter.

### Fixed
- Values the meter fills with all-ones when it has nothing to report no longer show up as numbers: throttle used to read 199 %, gear 15, and a faultless ABS could raise a false "ABS reports a fault".
- The ride replay (a debug tool) no longer drops the last frame of a file.
- The wheelie angle follows the same per-nibble mask as Rideology.

### Not yet tried on a bike
Everything in this release that involves a real meter, mostly in the same places as before:
- whether the 0x45 request on connect alone makes the meter send the riding log extension — if it does not, the debug switch puts it back;
- the numbers on the Bike screen: torque, brake pressures, ABS target, suspension damping, cruise control, rotation rates, wheel speeds. They are decoded exactly as Rideology decodes them, but no one has compared them with a dash while riding;
- **the refuel diary**: the detector was tried with a replay (gauge 1 → 5 bars, range 40 → 240 km, 10 L estimated on a 15 L tank) and on an emulator, never at a real pump. The litre estimate is a guess until you correct it;
- **the battery log**: the rule (0.4 V lost over three days of readings) is a reasonable guess, not a manufacturer's figure; the readings will only be useful after a few weeks;
- the records and the end-of-ride summary are the only parts that never touch the bike.

### Which APK
- **`KawaNav-v0.7.apk`**: the app for everyday use.
- **`KawaNav-v0.7-debug.apk`**: the same app with the test tools (hidden cards: meter icons, bike data, notification capture) and the guarded writing of ride settings and shift lamp; tap the Settings title seven times. Both are signed with the same key, so one installs over the other and back, keeping rides, refuels, battery readings, the maintenance log and settings.

### Install / update
Open the APK on the phone (Android 13+). It updates v0.4 and later in place and keeps rides, refuels, battery readings, the maintenance log, settings and offline maps. It installs next to Darthbob's KawaNav, not over it: keep only one of them connected to the bike. Known limitations: [README](https://github.com/jackyes/kawa-nav/blob/v0.7/README.md#-known-limitations).
