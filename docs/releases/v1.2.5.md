New icons and smoother tabs, the log export in the normal app, a clutch check after every ride, and the shift lamp and ride settings writes tried on a Z900. The bike's protocol is unchanged from 1.2.4.

### New

- **Material icons.** The hand-drawn icons are replaced by Google's Material Symbols (Apache 2.0, credited in the README).
- **Tabs fade in** over the tab before, instead of Android's own slide, also when you go back to a tab that was already open. The tab bar stays still.
- **Settings → About → "Export the log"**, in every build: KawaNav's log files go to Download/KawaNav, to send with a problem report. It used to be in the debug build only. The log holds Google Maps' directions with street names, the bike's Bluetooth address and the helmet's name, as the card says; the notification capture, which holds other apps' messages, stays in the debug build.
- **A clutch check on every ride.** In 3rd gear and up, the engine speed per km/h of the rear wheel is fixed while the clutch holds. Each ride now compares the seconds at half throttle or more with the same gear at light throttle and shows the result under Engine. A clutch that slips lets the engine rev ahead of the wheel: when that happens by more than 3% in two hard accelerations or more, a notification at the end of the ride says the clutch may be slipping and opens the ride. Settings → "Clutch warning" (on by default) turns the notification off; the ride still shows the check. Rides recorded before are worked out again when they are opened.
- **Shift lamp and ride settings, tried on a Z900.** Changing the dash shift lamp's engine speed, and riding mode, power, KTRC and KQS from the Bike tab, has been done on a 2026 Z900 and the dash took them. The cards and their confirmations no longer say the writes are untested.

### For testers (debug build)

The Field tests screen keeps only the rear tyre numbers: the clutch, shift lamp and ride settings tests are done.

### Not yet tried on a bike

The clutch check was worked out from the field test: roll-ons in 3rd to 6th on a Z900 showed no slip, and six recorded rides read 1–4% below the light-throttle gearing at full throttle, never more than 1.1% above. The warning itself has never fired on a real ride, since no clutch slipped.

A report should include Settings → About → "Export the log".
